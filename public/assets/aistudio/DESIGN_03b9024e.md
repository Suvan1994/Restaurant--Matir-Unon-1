## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the artisanal luxury of authentic Bengali gastronomic culture. It reconciles primeval village heritage—clay hearths, brass vessels, hand-woven linens, and slow-braised delicacies—with the architectural restraint of modern high-end hospitality. 

The aesthetic is Tactile Editorial Heritage:
- **Atmosphere:** Warm, slow-paced, aromatic, and distinctly ceremonial.
- **Visual Tone:** Grounded terracotta fired surfaces balanced by brushed mustard brass accents, organic off-white parchment negative space, and deep charcoal hearth contrasts.
- **Architectural Motifs:** Prominent parabolic curves, arched alcoves (Bengali *Chala* and *Khamar* vaulted profiles), and restrained metallic borders echoing heirloom Kansa (bell-metal) cutlery.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from traditional Bengali culinary instruments and cooking processes:

- **Primary (`#8B3A2B`, Burnished Clay Terracotta):** Represents the heart of the earthen stove (*Mati*). Used for primary calls to action, hero section accents, key navigation points, and brand crests. A supporting lighter terracotta (`#A44A3F`) is reserved for interactive hover states and expressive badges.
- **Secondary (`#D4A373`, Antiqued Brass / Shorshe):** Reflects aged brass tableware, mustard paste, and heirloom vessels. Applied to secondary interactive elements, decorative borders, subheadings, and status indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#E9C46A`, Raw Mustard Bloom):** High-gleam warm metallic gold. Used sparingly for micro-accents, star ratings, degustation course tags, and refined decorative rules.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F2`, Parchment Canvas):** The foundational backdrop across light modes, mimicking sun-dried rice paper and hand-spun muslin (*Jamdani* weave base).
- **Neutral Base (`#262626`, Smoked Charcoal Slate):** The color of smoldering sal wood and hearth ash. Delivers deep, high-contrast readability for body text, footers, deep alcove backgrounds, and structural framing.

Surface distribution rules:
- Dominant canvases default to `#FAF7F2` with subtle tonal paper texturing.
- High-order experiential cards (e.g., Signature Menus, Private Dining Bookings) shift inverted onto `#262626` backgrounds with `#FAF7F2` typography and `#E9C46A` borders.

## Typography

The typography couples the regal, high-contrast serif grace of Playfair Display with the clean geometric legibility of Inter (multilingual fallback paired seamlessly alongside Hind Siliguri for authentic Bengali glyphs).

Hierarchy & Application Rules:
- **Playfair Display:** Exclusively reserved for section headings, course titles, degustation chapter numerals, and quotes. Italics should be used intentionally on Bengali culinary terminology (e.g., *Ilish Paturi*, *Daab Chingri*, *Kosha Mangsho*).
- **Inter / Hind Siliguri:** Powers menus, ingredient narratives, reservation date-pickers, dietary markers, and UI controls. It provides neutral, unencumbered balance against the ornate display serif.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) to create an editorial, museum-catalog grade cadence.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a fluid 12-column grid system for desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single-column 4-track stack for mobile devices. 

Layout Model & Rhythm:
- **Canvas Boundaries:** Generous outer margins ensure the layout feels unhurried, echoing an elite culinary dining space with substantial breathing room.
- **Vertical Flow:** Editorial multi-tier rhythms: culinary imagery, narratives, and menu items should alternate between full-bleed arch hero segments and tightly aligned, asymmetrical columns.
- **Architectural Framing:** Image frames and featured recipe cards feature asymmetrical spacing where top paddings expand into arched baby-arcs while base paddings remain grounded.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold programmatic drop-shadows. Instead, elevation relies on warm, tinted atmospheric ambient shadows and tactile surface stacking:

1. **Earthen Stack (Low Elevation):** 
   - Border: 1px solid rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.25) (Antiqued Brass shimmer).
   - Shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(139, 58, 43, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(38, 38, 38, 0.04)`.
   - Used for dish display cards, wine pairings, and reservation step indicators.

2. **Alcove Elevation (Mid Elevation - Modals & Flyouts):**
   - Backdrop: Warm blurred parchment overlay (`rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
   - Shadow: `0 16px 36px -4px rgba(38, 38, 38, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(139, 58, 43, 0.08)`.
   - Used for table booking dialogues, wine selection cards, and tasting detail overlays.

3. **Brass Inlay Traces:**
   - Visual boundary lines use hairline 1px borders tinted in `#D4A373` at 30% to 50% opacity, replicating delicate brass-framed glass display vitrines.

## Shapes

The geometric signature of this design system is defined by parabolic curves and traditional Bengal temple arches (*Jor-Bangla* baby-arcs):

- **Asymmetric Vault Arches (`rounded-t-[4rem]` or `rounded-t-full`):** Signature food imagery containers, feature banner framing, and modal headers utilize tall top parabolic curves grounded on a flat 0.5rem base.
- **Standard UI Surfaces:** Form inputs, menu pills, and utility containers maintain level 2 roundedness (`0.5rem`), lending structural solidity without clashing against the dramatic arch motif.
- **Circular Embellishments:** Stamp medallions, seasonal seals, and chef insignia utilize pure `50%` circles nested inside hairline brass frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Reservations & Inquiries):** Deep Terracotta background (`#8B3A2B`), `#FAF7F2` text, 0.5rem rounded corners, framed with a 1px border of `#D4A373`. On hover: transition to `#A44A3F` with an expanding brass inner glow (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px #E9C46A`).
- **Secondary Action (View Menu, Our Heritage):** Transparent canvas, `#262626` text, 1px solid `#D4A373`. On hover: background fills with `rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.1)` and text shifts to `#8B3A2B`.
- **Minimal Text Link:** Playfair Display Italic, `#8B3A2B`, with a brass border underline placed 4px below the baseline that expands horizontally from center on hover.

### Cards & Menu Containers
- **Degustation / Dish Card:** Background `#FAF7F2` with top parabolic curve framing (`rounded-t-[4rem] rounded-b-lg`). Edge bound by a 1px brass tint (`rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.35)`). Image is masked by the upper baby-arc, revealing the dish within a vaulted alcove window.
- **Heirloom Charcoal Cards:** Inverted container for private dining or cellar archives. Background `#262626`, top border highlighted by a 2px `#E9C46A` gold thread rule, text in `#FAF7F2`, with sub-details in `#D4A373`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Inputs & Date-Pickers:** Parchment background (`#FAF7F2`), subtle bottom-only border (2px `#D4A373` at 40% opacity) or full soft enclosure (`rounded-md`, 1px `#D4A373`). On active focus: border deepens to `#8B3A2B` with zero harsh blue outlines; label shifts into an uppercase micro-label in `#8B3A2B`.

### Dietary & Course Chips
- **Status & Attribute Badges (e.g., Sattvic, Hilsa Season, Clay-Baked):** Pill containers (`rounded-full`), padded with `0.25rem 0.75rem`. Background tint of `#D4A373` at 15% opacity, text in `#8B3A2B`, uppercase tracking at `0.08em`.

### Lists & Menu Item Rows
- **Menu Line Item:** Two-column split with a dotted brass leader line (`border-b border-dotted border-[#D4A373]`) connecting the dish title and price. English title set in Playfair Display (`headline-sm`), with Bengali script (*Hind Siliguri*) or ingredient list set beneath in muted slate charcoal (`body-sm`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection Controls:** Custom circular inputs. Inactive state: 1.5px border of `#D4A373`. Active selected state: `#8B3A2B` filled dot surrounded by a 2px offset ring of raw mustard brass (`#E9C46A`).
